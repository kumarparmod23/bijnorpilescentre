  <h2>What Is Sclerotherapy?</h2>
  <p>Sclerotherapy is a minimally invasive treatment in which a sclerosing medicine is injected into selected internal hemorrhoidal tissue. The aim is to reduce bleeding and shrink the treated tissue.</p>

  <h2>Who May Be Suitable?</h2>
  <p>Sclerotherapy may be considered for selected patients, particularly when internal piles are causing bleeding and are suitable for injection-based treatment. The choice depends on the grade, size, symptoms and examination findings.</p>

  <h2>How Treatment Is Planned</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>Clinical assessment and proctological examination</li>
    <li>Assessment of hemorrhoid grade and symptoms</li>
    <li>Discussion of suitable treatment options</li>
    <li>Follow-up advice based on individual response</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>Other Treatment Options</h2>
  <p>Depending on the condition, treatment may include <a href="{{ '/en/kshar-karma-piles/' | relative_url }}">Kshar Karma</a>, <a href="{{ '/en/rubber-band-ligation/' | relative_url }}">Rubber Band Ligation</a>, lifestyle measures or other procedures.</p>

  <h2>98% Treatment Success Rate*</h2>
  <p>Our clinic reports a 98% treatment success rate*. Individual outcomes vary according to the condition, severity, treatment selected and patient factors.</p>

  <p class="text-xs text-slate-500">*Reported treatment success rate; individual results vary by condition and patient.</p>
